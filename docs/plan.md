## Project Summary 
The goal is to deliver a professional, high-converting website that serves as the digital storefront for **Reshape Your Reality**, a premium personal training service in Houston,TX.

This site will focus on credibility, lead generation (Free Consult Booking), and fast performance while staying within a clean, modern design. 

## TAME Risk Analysis
**Threats**
- Delayed Content/Asset delivery
- Scope Creep (Request for booking systems blog, etc.)
- Design approval takes longer
- Technical Issues (Performance)

**Assumptions**
- Client will provide all content and logos promptly
- No custom photography or video required
- Business Owner available for weekly reviews

**Mitigations**
- Clear escalation path for risks

**Escalations**
- Major delay in content delivery 
- Budget Overrun risk
